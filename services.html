How I work
01 Brief You tell me what you need, who it's for and when you need it. The more you share, the better the result.
02 Agree We agree the details in writing: what I'll make, the timeline, the price, the rounds of revisions and how payment works. Work starts once you confirm.
03 Direction I explore ideas and share a creative direction with you for feedback before going deeper.
04 Design & refine I develop the chosen direction and refine it through the agreed rounds of revisions.
05 Deliver Once the project is paid for in full, you receive the final files in the formats we agreed, with the rights to use the work for its agreed purpose.
Pricing

Every project is quoted individually, so you only pay for what you need.

The price depends on:

  • Scope — what you need and how many pieces
  • Revisions — the rounds agreed, plus any extra changes
  • Timeline — how soon you need it
  • Licensed materials — any fonts, stock images or music that cost money, which I'll tell you about first

Frequently asked questions
What services do you offer?

Graphic design, brand identity, social media design, flyers and posters, UI/UX design, photo editing, motion graphics and video editing. The Portfolio page shows examples of each.

How does a project start?

Send me a message through the contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp and tell me what you need. Before any work begins we agree the details in writing: what I'll make, the timeline, the price, the rounds of revisions and how payment works.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually, based on what you need. Tell me about your project and I'll send you a quote.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the project. We agree the timeline in writing before work starts.

How many revisions are included?

The rounds of revisions we agree in your quote are included in the price. Changes beyond that, or a change of direction after approval, count as extra work and I'll quote them before starting.

What do you need from me?

A clear brief, the materials I ask for (text, logos, photos, brand guidelines), permission to use anything you send me, and feedback in good time. Delays on your side can move the delivery date.

Who owns the final design?

Once a project is paid for in full, you receive the rights to use the final delivered work for the purposes we agreed. Source files, sketches and unused ideas stay with me unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Can you show my project in your portfolio?

Unless you ask me not to, I may show the finished work in my portfolio and on my social media. If your project needs to stay private, tell me before we start.

What file formats will I get?

We agree the formats in your quote, based on where the work will be used, such as web, print or social media.

What software do you use?

Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Figma and Blender.

What happens to the information I send you?

I use it only to reply to you and to work on your project. I don't sell it or use it for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
