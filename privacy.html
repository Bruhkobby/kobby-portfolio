Privacy

What this website collects, and what I do with it. Last updated 17 October 2026.

01 — Overview

This page explains what personal information this website collects, why, and what happens to it. The short version: I collect only what you choose to send me, I use it to reply to you, and I don't sell it or use it for advertising.

"I", "me" and "Kobby" refer to the designer behind this website. You can reach me at bruhkobby707@gmail.com.

02 — What I collect

The only form on this site is the contact form. If you use it, I receive and store:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message, including anything you choose to tell me about your project

If you contact me another way (email, phone, or social media), I receive whatever details you send, and the service you use may also process them.

There are no user accounts, no newsletter sign-ups and no payment forms on this site. I don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools to follow what you do on it.

03 — How I use it

I use your details to:

  • Read and reply to your enquiry
  • Discuss, quote and deliver a project if we work together
  • Keep a record of our conversation and agreement

I don't sell your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

04 — Who else handles it

To run this site I rely on a few services. They handle data on my behalf or when your browser loads the site:

  • Supabase stores contact-form messages and the images and details of the projects shown on this site. The database is hosted in London (UK).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like most web hosts, it may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them directly from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • jsDelivr delivers a code library the site needs to talk to the database.
  • YouTube and Vimeo play videos embedded in some project pages. They may set their own cookies if you press play. Their privacy policies apply to that.
  • Your email provider and mine carry any emails we exchange.

Apart from these, I only share your information if the law requires me to.

05 — Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies or use trackers for visitors. The only data the site stores in your browser is a sign-in session for the private admin area, which only I use. Embedded videos (see above) are the one place third-party cookies can appear, and only if you play them.

06 — How long I keep it

I keep messages for as long as I need them to handle your enquiry and any project that follows, and to keep reasonable business records. If you'd like me to delete your messages sooner, just ask.

07 — Your choices

You can ask me at any time to:

  • Tell you what information I hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information

Email bruhkobby707@gmail.com and I'll respond as soon as I can. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

08 — Children

This site is aimed at businesses and creators, not children. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent me their details, let me know and I'll delete them.

09 — Changes

If I change how the site handles information, I'll update this page and the date at the top. This version was last updated on 17 October 2026.
